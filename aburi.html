Aburi Botanical Gardens

Eastern Region · Akuapem Hills · 30 km north of Accra

★ 4.5198 reviews

About

Opened in 1890 on the site of a former sanatorium, Aburi covers around 64 hectares of the Akuapem ridge. The avenue of royal palms at the entrance is the best-known feature, but the collection runs to hundreds of species across the terraces.

The hills sit several hundred metres above Accra and are noticeably cooler, which is most of the reason city residents come up at weekends. Craft stalls line the road into town, and the drive up through Peduase is worth the trip on its own.
