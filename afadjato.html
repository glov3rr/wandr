Mount Afadja

Volta Region · Liati Wote · the highest peak in Ghana

★ 4.9147 reviews

About

Mount Afadja, usually called Afadjato, rises to about 885 metres on the Ghana–Togo border and is the highest point in the country. The climb starts from the village of Liati Wote, where the community runs the trail and provides guides.

It is steep rather than long — most people reach the summit in 90 minutes to two hours. The last section is a scramble through scrub. On a clear morning the view runs across the Volta hills and well into Togo.

Good to know

Getting there
From Hohoe to Liati Wote, then the trail starts in the village
Climb
90 minutes to two hours up, about an hour down
Difficulty
Steep and unshaded in places — take more water than you think
Guides
Arranged in the village, fees support the community
Best time
Start at first light, before the heat
Nearby
Tagbo Falls is a short walk from the same village
