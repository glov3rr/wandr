Cape Coast Castle

Central Region · Cape Coast · UNESCO World Heritage Site

★ 4.7538 reviews

About

Cape Coast Castle began as a Swedish trading post in 1653 and passed through Danish and Dutch hands before the British took it in 1664 and made it the headquarters of their administration on the Gold Coast. For nearly two centuries it was one of the largest holding sites in the transatlantic slave trade.

The tour moves between the airy governor's quarters above and the dungeons below, ending at the Door of No Return that opens onto the shore. It is a difficult visit and deliberately so. A museum on the upper floor covers Akan history and the wider trade.
