Elmina Castle

Central Region · Elmina · UNESCO World Heritage Site

★ 4.6391 reviews

About

Built by the Portuguese in 1482 as São Jorge da Mina, Elmina is the oldest surviving European building in sub-Saharan Africa. The Dutch captured it in 1637 and held it for over two centuries before ceding it to the British in 1872.

The whitewashed fort sits directly above a working fishing harbour, and the contrast between the two is part of the visit. Tours cover the courtyards, the church the Dutch built inside the walls, and the dungeons beneath.
