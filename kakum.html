Kakum National Park

Central Region · 33 km north of Cape Coast

★ 4.8412 reviews

About

Kakum protects one of the last stretches of undisturbed rainforest in southern Ghana, covering roughly 375 square kilometres of the Central Region. The park is best known for its canopy walkway, a series of rope bridges suspended between hardwood trees and crossed on foot around thirty metres above the forest floor.

Below the canopy the forest supports forest elephants, bongo antelope, several primate species and over two hundred kinds of bird. Most visitors come for the walkway and the guided ground trails, both of which start from the visitor centre at the park entrance near Abrafo.

Good to know

Getting there
Tro-tro or taxi from Cape Coast, roughly 45 minutes
Canopy walk
Seven bridges, about 40 minutes end to end
Guides
Compulsory on all trails, arranged at the visitor centre
Best time
Early morning, before the tour groups arrive
Bring
Closed shoes, water, insect repellent
Nearby
Often combined with Cape Coast and Elmina in one day
