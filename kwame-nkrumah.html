Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park

Greater Accra · Central Accra · mausoleum and museum

★ 4.6286 reviews

About

The park stands on the old polo grounds where Kwame Nkrumah declared Ghana's independence in March 1957. It now holds the mausoleum of Ghana's first president and his wife Fathia, set behind a long avenue of fountains.

A museum below the monument covers Nkrumah's life, his role in pan-Africanism, and the 1966 coup that removed him. Personal effects, photographs and correspondence are on display, alongside the original statue toppled during the coup.
