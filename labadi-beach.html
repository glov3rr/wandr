Labadi Beach

Greater Accra · La, Accra · also called La Pleasure Beach

★ 4.6502 reviews

About

Labadi is the busiest beach in Ghana and the most reliably lively stretch of the Accra coast. Weekends bring live highlife and reggae bands, drumming groups, horse riding along the sand and a steady run of food stalls behind the beach.

It is a social beach rather than a quiet one. The surf has a strong undertow and swimming is best kept close to shore. Several hotels along the strip maintain their own sections with loungers and calmer crowds.

Good to know

Getting there
About 20 minutes east of central Accra
Entry
A gate fee applies, higher at weekends
Weekends
Live music from the afternoon onwards
Swimming
Strong undertow — stay near the shore
Food
Grilled tilapia and kelewele from the stalls behind the sand
Best time
Sunday afternoon for atmosphere, weekday morning for quiet
