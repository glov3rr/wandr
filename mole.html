Mole National Park

Savannah Region · Near Damongo · Ghana's largest national park

★ 4.7243 reviews

About

Mole covers roughly 4,577 square kilometres of guinea savannah in northern Ghana, making it by far the country's largest protected area. Elephants are the draw, and they are regularly seen at the waterholes below the escarpment where the park accommodation sits.

Unusually, Mole allows walking safaris with an armed ranger as well as vehicle drives. Buffalo, warthog, kob, roan antelope and several primate species are common; the park also records over three hundred bird species.

Good to know

Getting there
About two hours by road from Tamale to Damongo
Safaris
Walking and vehicle drives, both ranger-led
Best time
Dry season, roughly November to April, when animals gather at water
Timing
Drives leave at dawn and late afternoon
Stay
Book accommodation inside the park well ahead
Bring
Neutral clothing, binoculars, sun protection
