Shai Hills Resource Reserve

Greater Accra · Near Doryumu · 50 km from Accra

★ 4.7164 reviews

About

Shai Hills is the closest wildlife reserve to Accra, a stretch of grassland and inselbergs covering around 51 square kilometres. Olive baboons are everywhere and unbothered by visitors; kob, bushbuck and introduced zebra are also present.

The hills themselves hold caves once occupied by the Shai people before their removal in 1892, and several are open to visitors with a guide. The rock outcrops are climbable and give a long view across the Accra plains.
